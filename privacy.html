Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Welcome to PixelForge ("we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website (https://pixelkit.org) and our free image editing tools.

1. Information We Collect

PixelForge processes all images directly in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to our servers. All editing, compression, OCR text extraction, and QR scanning happens locally on your device.

However, like most websites, we may collect non-personally identifying information such as:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring website
  • Date and time of visit
  • Pages viewed
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level)

This information is collected automatically through standard web server logs and is used to understand how visitors use our site and to improve the user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Operate and maintain our website
  • Understand how users interact with our tools
  • Improve and optimize our services
  • Detect and prevent technical issues or abuse
  • Display relevant advertisements (see Section 4 below)

3. Cookies

A cookie is a string of information that a website stores on a visitor's computer, and that the visitor's browser provides to the website each time the visitor returns. We use cookies to help us identify and track visitors, their usage of our website, and their website access preferences.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our website properly. Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our site. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Third-Party Links and Services

Our website may contain affiliate links (such as Canva) or links to third-party websites. If you click on these links and make a purchase or take an action, we may earn a commission. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

6. Data Security

The security of your information is important to us. Because your images are processed locally in your browser and never leave your device, the security risk is minimal. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect our website and any collected data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • By visiting our Contact Page
  • By email: support@pixelkit.org

PixelForge is committed to protecting your privacy. All image processing happens locally in your browser — your files never leave your device.
