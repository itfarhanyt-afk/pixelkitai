About PixelForge

PixelForge is a completely free, browser-based image editing platform. We believe powerful image editing tools should be accessible to everyone — no signups, no uploads to servers, and no hidden costs.

✨ What We Offer

  • 📝 Image to Text (OCR) - Extract text from any image
  • 📱 QR Code Scanner - Decode QR codes instantly
  • 🗜 Compress - Reduce file size without losing quality
  • ↔ Resize - Change dimensions with aspect ratio lock
  • ✂ Crop - Smart cropping with presets
  • 🔄 Convert - Switch between JPG, PNG, and WEBP
  • 🎨 Effects - Grayscale, sepia, and invert filters
  • 💧 Watermark - Add custom text watermarks

🔒 Privacy First

Unlike many online tools, PixelForge processes everything directly in your browser. Your images never leave your device — no uploads to external servers, no data collection, no privacy concerns.

💡 Why We Built This

The original pixelkit.org went offline, leaving users without access to free image editing tools. We rebuilt and improved it from scratch, making it faster, more feature-rich, and completely free — forever.
