About PixelForge

What is PixelForge?

PixelForge is a free, privacy-focused online image processing tool. We offer image compression, resizing, cropping, format conversion, background removal, watermarking, and more — all directly in your browser.

Our Mission

To provide powerful image editing tools that are:

✅ Completely free — No hidden costs or subscriptions
✅ Private — Your images never leave your device
✅ Fast — Processing happens instantly in your browser
✅ Easy to use — No design skills needed

How It Works

Unlike other image tools, PixelForge processes everything locally. When you upload an image, it stays in your browser. We don't have servers that store your images. This means:

🔒 Your privacy is protected
⚡ Lightning-fast processing
🌐 Works even with slow internet

Who Created PixelForge?

PixelForge was created by a solo developer who believes powerful image editing tools should be accessible to everyone, without subscriptions or privacy concerns.

Why "PixelForge"?

The name combines "Pixel" (the building block of digital images) with "Forge" (a place where metal is shaped and crafted). We help you shape and craft your images into exactly what you need.
