Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-06-26

1. Information We Collect
PixelKit operates entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The only data stored are your preferences (language, theme, and login status) using your browser's local storage. No personal information is required to use our tools.

2. Usage Data
We anonymously track which tools are used and how many files are processed, but this information is stored only on your device (in local storage) and is never sent to any server. It is used solely to display your session dashboard.

3. Third-Party Libraries
To provide OCR, QR scanning, and AI background removal, we load scripts from trusted CDNs (jsDelivr, unpkg). These scripts may set their own technical cookies essential for their functionality. We do not control these cookies; please refer to the respective privacy policies of the library providers if concerned.

4. Cookies
PixelKit does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. Functional cookies (e.g., for maintaining your login state within the simulated account system) may be stored in local storage, not HTTP cookies. You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to remove all data.

5. Data Security
Since all processing occurs client-side, your images never leave your device. We implement best practices to ensure the security of the local processing environment.

6. Children's Privacy
Our service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

8. Contact Us
If you have questions, please use the contact form.
